This Privacy Policy explains how Timer Pro (“the App”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App on Android.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1) Information We Collect
A) Information you create in the App

The App stores the information you enter and manage inside the App, such as:

Timers and timer settings

Routines and routine steps

App preferences (notifications, reminders, widgets, etc.)

History/usage logs created by the App (for insights and stats)

Backups you export/import (if you use the backup feature)

This data is primarily stored locally on your device.

B) Device and app data (limited)

The App may process limited technical data for reliability, such as:

App version/build information

Basic device/OS info required for compatibility

Error logs generated by the App (if logging is enabled in the App)

C) Permissions-based data (only when you enable a feature)

The App may request optional Android permissions. If you grant permission, the App can access related data strictly for that feature:

Notifications: To show timer/routine reminders and alerts.

Calendar access (optional): If you enable calendar integration, the App may read calendar event data to support scheduling or integration features.

Location access (optional): If you enable location triggers/geofencing, the App may use your device location to trigger routines/actions based on your configured rules. Location is used only for the trigger functionality you enable.

Storage / Files (optional): If you export or import backups or export insights (CSV/PDF), the App may use file access to read/write the file you select.

Text-to-Speech (optional): If you enable spoken prompts, the App uses Android’s TTS services to speak content.

You can deny permissions. Some features may not work without them.

2) How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

Provide app functionality (timers, routines, history, reminders)

Schedule and display notifications you configured

Provide insights/statistics based on your usage

Import/export backups and exports when you request it

Improve stability, diagnose crashes, and fix bugs

We do not sell your personal information.

3) Data Storage and Security
A) Local storage

Most of your app data is stored locally on your device using Android storage mechanisms.

B) Backups and exports

If you export a backup or export insights, the exported file is saved where you choose (device storage or a location you select via Android’s file picker/share sheet).
You are responsible for keeping exported files secure.

C) Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect rights and safety.
